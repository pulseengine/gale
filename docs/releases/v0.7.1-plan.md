# v0.7.1 — "Reach" (plan)

The breadth half of the v0.7.0 split. v0.7.0 tests one claim deeply — that the composed OS
is one OS, and what it costs once dissolved. **v0.7.1 widens the surface, and every item
here depends on v0.7.0's E2 having produced a real object first.**

## Why these were moved here

v0.7.0's plan said *"new drivers, new capabilities and new targets all wait behind E1"*,
while the rivet scope typed in #244 put a third target family, ARINC component tenants and
driver componentization inside v0.7.0. Both were committed; both could not govern. The
release was split on 2026-08-05 rather than one silently overruling the other.

The argument for the split is that the breadth items multiply unverified surface on an OS
that had never been executed. Once E1 has run it and E2 has measured it, the same work is
being added to something known rather than something assumed.

## Scope

| artifact | what it converts |
|---|---|
| `REQ-OS-TARGET-RV32-001` / `VER-…` | RISC-V from *compiled* to *executed* — a model-generated RV32 target plus one driver silicon-validated against real registers |
| `REQ-OS-PARTITION-TENANT-001` / `VER-…` | ARINC-653 partitions from modelled to enforced — a component tenant confined to its partition on real silicon |
| `REQ-DRV-COMPONENT-001` / `VER-…` | every thin-seam driver to a wasm component, with a whole-graph no-env-import gate |

Plus the two execution rungs that could not fit v0.7.0's cut:

- **E3** — run the dissolved composite under qemu / Renode; content gates on the composed
  OS, not only per-provider.
- **E4** — run it on silicon (G474, F100), recorded with its `n`, its unspliced-capture
  status, and what it does *not* evidence, in the style of `silicon/RESULTS-wdg-f100.md`.

## The question this release has to answer

`REQ-OS-PARTITION-TENANT-001` carries the open review question from Research Day:

> what enforces isolation once `meld fuse --memory shared` collapses shared-nothing linking
> into one address space?

v0.6.0 modelled partitioning; it is not enforced on silicon. This is where that gap closes
or is restated with a reason. Answering it with a model rather than a fault on a real MPU
would be the failure mode.
