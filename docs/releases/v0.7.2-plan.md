# v0.7.2 — "Measure It" (plan)

The timing triad, re-pointed here from v0.7.0 on 2026-08-26.

v0.7.0 was scoped as *"Run It, Then Measure It"*. It ran it — E1 observed a handle
crossing `spawn -> timer -> exec`, E2 produced the first native object for the whole
OS — and those results are verified and shipped. The measuring half reached **51%**
(T2) and **3 of 31** (T4), and both remainders are in other repositories.

This release is that remainder, given its own identity instead of being folded into
the breadth release or holding a finished depth release hostage.

## Why re-pointed rather than deferred

This is the second time the triad has moved. The first time, at v0.6.0, it was
deferred **without re-pointing** — so a shipped tag kept querying as unclosed and
six real work items were invisible to any plan. That is recorded in
`v0.7.0-plan.md` as the reason the release was split on 2026-08-05.

So the mechanism matters more than the decision. All six artifacts carry
`release: v0.7.2` explicitly. They remain in every readiness query; nothing is
hidden, and no shipped tag queries as unclosed.

## What is already known, so this release does not re-derive it

**T2 — the gap is control flow, and it has no obligation to be missing.**
`BrIf` (40), `Block` (38), `End` (64), `Call` (31), `Br` (7), `Unreachable` (6) plus
byte-level memory (`I32Load8U`/`I32Store8`, 34) carry neither a Rocq obligation nor
SMT verification. synth established *why*: `coq/Synth/WASM/WasmInstructions.v`'s
`wasm_instr` inductive has **no such constructor** — the obligations are not missing,
they are unstatable until the model's syntax is extended. Scoped upstream as
**synth#1057**, `BrIf` first, under `VCR-WASM-001` for synth v0.60.

Denominator pinned by committed fixtures (`measurements/verify-reports/*.verify.json`)
because a clean rebuild did **not** reproduce the first figure: 477 -> 496 applied,
52%/48% -> 51%/49%. `verified` reproduced exactly (22); the drift was entirely in
`declined`, and its cause is that the seam stubs are rebuilt from source with nothing
pinning them.

**T4 — the blocker inverted once and then held.**
On synth 0.52.0 the decline set read `unmodeled-op` 9 vs `loop` 8, which made the
cycle model look like the critical path. synth#936 closed that; on 0.57.0 it reads
`unmodeled-op` 2 vs **`loop` 13**, with **11 `callee-unbounded` cascades** behind the
leaves. So the plan's original single named blocker — scry loop-bound inference —
turned out right after a cycle of looking wrong.

Routed as **scry#144**, accepted; scry will emit `loop_bounds` against synth's
existing `--wcet-hints` seam (`synth-wcet-hints-v1`), where hints are **untrusted and
re-checked** by synth's own loop-induction proof rather than trusted across the seam.

**A join-key blocker sits under T4.** 7 of the 13 `loop` declines are `func_<index>`:
`synth-wcet-v1` names functions from the export section only (measured — all 23 named
entries are exports, all 8 anonymous are not). A hint cannot be keyed to them, and one
written against `func_22` retargets silently when indices shift. **synth#1063**.
Fixture: `measurements/wcet-fixture/`.

**T3 — one instantiation is unsound, and it passes short tests.**
spar#331 delivered `ArincSupply.lean` (0 `sorry`/`admit`/`axiom`): blocking folds into
release jitter, `lsbf` is monotone, and soundness is conditional on an explicit
`SupplyGuarantee` hypothesis spar deliberately does **not** prove — it is the partition
scheduler's obligation, i.e. gale's.

`Switcher::tick` fires at `t == end - 1`, so each owned window loses its final tick to
the switch. A partition owning `k` windows gets **`Θ_eff = Θ − k`**, and instantiating
with the raw budget is **unsound**: `lsbf`'s leading rate is `Θ/Π` against a real rate
of `(Θ−k)/Π`. Exhaustive simulation over all worst-case start offsets:

| frame | Π | Θ | k | first violation, raw Θ | with Θ_eff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| one window | 100 | 40 | 1 | **t = 2 560** | none ≤ 6 000 |
| two windows | 100 | 50 | 2 | **t = 1 926** | none ≤ 6 000 |
| three windows | 100 | 70 | 3 | **t = 828** | none ≤ 6 000 |

More windows fails *sooner* — so splitting a budget to reduce blackout, the natural
design move, makes the wrong instantiation fail faster.

## Ordering

    T4  scry#144 + synth#1063 ──► C_i exist ──► T3 demand half
    T3  supply half  ── gale-owned, needs no C_i, startable now
    T2  synth#1057   ── independent of both

**T3's supply half is the only part startable without upstream delivery.** It also
carries a side condition Lean cannot discharge: `Θ_eff = Θ − k` assumes the three-atom
switch sequence completes within one tick — a WCET obligation on trusted native code,
outside both BIN-VERIFY and MC/DC. It must be stated as a hypothesis, in exactly the
shape spar chose for `SupplyGuarantee`.

## Kill-criteria

- **T2** — `REQ-OS-OBJVERIFY-001` demands **zero** coverage gap over the union. Anything
  short of 496 of 496 fails it. Partial progress is reported as a number, never as
  "substantially covered".
- **T4** — a bound that does not dominate every observed DWT high-water-mark falsifies
  it. **No partition budget may be sized from a raw DWT observation**; DWT only ever
  falsifies the model.
- **T3** — the Lean proof fails to discharge, or an observed high-water-mark exceeds
  the machine-checked bound.

## Precondition

**#286/#288** — gale's `proofs/lean/` was never compiled by any workflow, so T3's
kill-criterion could not fire. The `lean` job must be green before any T3 Lean work
counts as evidence.
